Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

ACEYA App ("the app") is a private community app for the Mar Patros Parish youth group. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, date of birth, phone number, and optionally a profile picture. We also store attendance records, volunteer sign-ups, group chat messages, direct messages, and prayer requests that you create within the app.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to provide the app's features: tracking your attendance, letting other members see your profile, enabling messaging between members, and showing your participation on leaderboards within the community. Your attendance percentage and volunteer points are visible to other signed-in members on in-app leaderboards.

Who Can See Your Information

Your profile (name, picture, role, attendance percentage, volunteer points) is visible to other signed-in members of the Mar Patros Parish youth community. Admins can see all member data. Your data is not sold or shared with any third parties outside the app.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Account and content data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Storage). Firebase handles data encryption in transit and at rest.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: tap the menu icon, tap Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete My Account." This permanently removes your profile and all associated data. No data is retained after deletion.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for parish youth members. Users under 13 should only use the app with parental or guardian consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact Athan Youkhana at athanyoukhana@gmail.com.
